A LITTLE CLARITY

Before we hit
the trail.

Is this right for my dog?

The launch is designed for beginner dogs and owners seeking everyday foundations. The introduction helps assess goals, comfort, and suitability. Serious aggression, severe reactivity, or complex behavioral needs require referral to a qualified specialist.

Where do sessions happen?

At a suitable client location or explicitly permitted training space in the service area. The exact location is agreed individually before an appointment is confirmed.

Do I need experience?

No prior training experience is needed for beginner foundations. You’ll practice clear cues and rewards together, with short assignments between sessions.

Can I book online now?

Use the Calendly section below to book your free introduction when appointments open. You can reschedule or cancel through the links in your Calendly confirmation. The optional Foundations program is discussed after your introduction; it cannot be booked or paid for online.
